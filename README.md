### CHEM-000228: Uniform first-ionization-energy bound
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Prove that \(E_Z(Z-1)-E_Z(Z)\le C\) for neutral atoms, uniformly in nuclear charge, with the complete electron–electron interaction.
- **Status:** A longstanding component of the ionization conjecture. Solovej proved the Hartree–Fock version; the full Schrödinger problem remains open because available total-energy asymptotics do not control the required order-one difference.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Places the bounded energy scale of outer-electron chemistry on a many-body foundation.
- **Key works:** J. P. Solovej (2003), *The Ionization Conjecture in Hartree-Fock Theory*, [arXiv:math-ph/0012026](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0012026); M. Lewin (2025), *Some open mathematical problems concerning charged quantum particles*, [DOI:10.5802/crphys.249](https://doi.org/10.5802/crphys.249).
- **Related:** Mathematical physics: semiclassical asymptotics with order-one remainder control.
